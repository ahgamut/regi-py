    regi-py

    An open-source implementation of the card game Regicide. The engine and the neural-net bots run entirely in your browser as WebAssembly — nothing is sent to a server.

    GitHub Full rules (PDF)

    How to play

    Goal. You and your party cooperate to defeat all 12 royals — the Jacks, Queens and Kings — in order. Clear every royal to win; if any player can't survive a royal's attack, the whole party loses.

    Each royal has health (damage needed to defeat it) and an attack value (damage it deals back). On your turn you attack, then defend against the counter-strike.

    In this app you take one seat; the other seats are neural-net bots running locally.

    Attack. Play a combo from your hand to damage the current royal. The damage dealt equals the total pip value of the cards you play.

    Playing a suit also triggers its power (equal to the cards' value):

    ♥ Hearts — heal: shuffle that many discards back under the deck.
    ♦ Diamonds — draw: the party draws that many cards.
    ♣ Clubs — double: the attack's damage counts twice.
    ♠ Spades — shield: lower the royal's attack for the rest of the fight.

    You may Yield (play nothing) when it's allowed — hover Submit with no card selected and it becomes Yield.

    Defend. After your attack the royal strikes back for its attack value. You must discard cards whose total value covers the incoming damage.

    Accumulated block (from Spades played this fight) reduces the incoming damage first — if block already covers it, you take no hit. If you can't cover what's left, you fall and the party loses.

    A legal combo is one of:

    • A single card.
    • An Ace + one other card — their values add together.
    • Two to four cards of the same rank, of different suits, whose values sum to ≤ 10 (e.g. two 4s, or 2+3+5).

    Playing a multi-card combo triggers every suit power among its cards. In this app, click the cards you want, then press Submit — it only lights up when your selection is a legal combo.
